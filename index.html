THE ARTIST

Warren Will is a Munich-based DJ and producer developing a deeper sense for house music in the Munich DJ scene. His sound is groovy, bass-driven, and built for movement — from intimate club floors to open-air mountain stages.

From remixing Kanye West and Ace of Base to recording live Afro House sets at Graf Rumford Munich, Warren bridges technical studio craft with raw dancefloor instinct. Every set tells a story. Every track is an invitation.

"Developing a deeper sense for house music to gain attention in the DJ scene."

— Warren Will, YouTube bio
